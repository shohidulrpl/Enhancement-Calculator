What is the Enhancement Calculator?

The Enhancement Calculator (also known as the Credit Enhancement Calculator or Enhance Calculator) is a free, browser-based tool that instantly calculates a customer's credit enhancement value based on their average transaction value over the last 90 days. It supports 30-day, 45-day, and 60-day constraint periods used by finance and collection teams.

How to Use the Credit Enhancement Calculator

  1. Enter the customer's last 90 days average value.
  2. Select a days constraint — 30, 45, or 60 days.
  3. Click Calculate Enhancement to view the result.
  4. Copy the result with one click and paste it into your workflow.

Enhancement Calculator Formulas

  • 30 Days: Average × 2.5
  • 45 Days: (Average × 1.5) × 2.5
  • 60 Days: (Average × 2) × 2.5
